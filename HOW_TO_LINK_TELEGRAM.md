# 📱 How to Link Your Telegram Account

**For Parents & Teachers**

Get instant OTP codes via Telegram instead of waiting for emails!

---

## ⚡ Quick Setup (3 Easy Steps)

### Step 1: Get Your Linking Code

1. Log in to your Spring of Knowledge Academy account
2. Go to **Account Settings**
3. Click the **"Link Telegram Account"** button
4. You'll see an 8-character code like: **`ABC123XY`**

### Step 2: Open Telegram

**Option A - Click the Link (Easiest!)**
- Just click the blue Telegram link shown
- Your Telegram app will open automatically
- The bot will link your account instantly

**Option B - Manual**
1. Open Telegram on your phone or computer
2. Search for: `@SpringKnowledgeOTP_bot`
3. Click on the bot
4. Send this message: `/start ABC123XY` (use your code)

### Step 3: Done! ✅

You'll get a confirmation message:

```
🎉 Account Linked Successfully!

You'll now receive OTP codes via Telegram!
```

## ❓ Common Questions

### Q: Do I have to use Telegram?

No! If you don't link Telegram, you'll still get OTP codes by email like before.

### Q: Is it safe?

Yes! Very safe:
- Telegram uses end-to-end encryption
- We only store your chat ID (no messages)
- We never ask for passwords via Telegram
- You can unlink anytime

### Q: What if I don't have Telegram?

1. Download Telegram (it's free):
   - iPhone: App Store
   - Android: Play Store
   - Computer: telegram.org
2. Create an account
3. Then follow the steps above

### Q: My code expired. What now?

Codes expire after 15 minutes for security.
Just generate a new code from Account Settings.

### Q: Can I unlink later?

Yes! Go to Account Settings and click "Unlink Telegram Account"

## 🆘 Need Help?

3. Check that your code hasn't expired (15 minutes)
4. Contact school administration if still having issues
